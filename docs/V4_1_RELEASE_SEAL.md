# ICTC V4.1 Experimental · Enterprise Nexus — release seal

## Outcome
V4.1 is a **presentation-only epistemic refinement** of the merged V4 Enterprise Nexus. It does not add a process, store, authority, decision type or evidence engine. It standardizes what business users see first and moves specialist lineage/proof terminology into progressive disclosure.

## Canonical business vocabulary
- RN-01 — Monitoraggio normativo
- EC-01 — Eventi e segnalazioni
- AO-01 — Inventario di sistemi e oggetti
- MC-01 — Controlli e copertura
- AP-01 — Azioni correttive
- RC-01 — Rischi di compliance
- AR-01 — Questionari e verifiche
- EV-01 — Prove e tracciabilità (support surface, not an eighth business service)

## Screen-by-screen intent
- **Oggi** — see processes first, continue urgent work, or use a secondary guided entry.
- **Processi** — choose a known compliance process directly and understand purpose, next step and proof.
- **RN-01 / EC-01 / AO-01 / MC-01 / AP-01 / RC-01 / AR-01** — use process-specific language and one clear primary action.
- **Prove** — reconstruct what was recorded, decided and supported without implying certification.
- **Trace** — show business sections first; hashes, raw epistemic structures and receipt details remain available under technical disclosure.

## Semantic wording correction
`AP-01 Completa` is presented as **Invia a verifica** when execution is finished but verified closure has not yet occurred. This aligns UI wording with the existing V4 separation between execution and human verification; it does not change the action state machine.
