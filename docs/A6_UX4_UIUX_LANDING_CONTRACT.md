# A6-UX4 landing-page UI/UX contract

This contract describes repository-owned presentation, not legal compliance or representative human-usability evidence.

## Home
One product identity, one priority queue, compact process icon and micro-open affordance. The user can answer what deserves attention and where to go next without interpreting an arrow as a business action.

## Processi di Compliance
Seven canonical peer procedures, stable codes and names, direct entry, no AI prerequisite and no competing navigation ontology.

## RN-01 — Monitoraggio normativo e fonti
One visible operational collection per local record family, actionable-by-default scope, full-width source work, contribution entry retained, redundant `Materiali in ingresso` aside retired. Source observation, verification and impact remain epistemically distinct.

## EC-01 — Incidenti e quasi incidenti
One `Eventi registrati` heading/count, one local filter toolbar and actionable-by-default native records. Registration does not imply external notification or legal qualification.

## AO-01 — Inventario di sistemi e oggetti
Native object registry is the visible work surface; S1/A1 worklist remains semantic authority. Candidate/governed/terminal lifecycle stays distinct from substantive completeness.

## MC-01 — Standard e Controlli
Organizational use, applicability and mapping remain separate axes. `undeclared` is presented as `Uso da dichiarare`; method evidence is not certification. Standard Browser is read-only, deduplicates equal ref/label identities and has no horizontal overflow at 1440/390/320.

## AP-01 — Azioni correttive
Native actions are the visible collection; proposed/open/in-progress/ready-for-review semantics remain typed and the UI does not convert navigation into execution.

## RC-01 — Rischi di compliance
Native risks are the visible collection; assessment, treatment and review remain human-governed and risk labels are not probabilities or legal conclusions.

## AR-01 — Richieste e assurance
Native assurance cases are the visible collection; drafting/review/approval remain separate and read-only actors cannot acquire write authority through presentation.

## Evidenze ICTC
Semantic evidence, decisions, bases and reconstruction remain primary. Role visibility is labelled `Vista per ruolo`; chain status is `Coerente tecnicamente`. Technical runtime snapshots remain secondary evidence and never imply compliance, deployment effectiveness or certification.

## Cross-surface anti-regression
A6-UX4 may suppress the separate visible worklist only if every actionable typed target binds to a native record. Any partial binding fails closed to the canonical visible worklist. The dedicated rail runs source contracts, 10k semantic antagonists, an additional 100k screenshot-derived UI/UX mutation campaign and exact-head Chromium oracles. Parent S4-A6 remains open.
