# DECIDE-0 — Evidence-bounded decision lattice

## Purpose
DECIDE-0 converts the merged SCOPE-0 target and REALITY-0 rehearsal into bounded implementation authority. It is prospective control-plane authority: it does not itself change business runtime behavior, API semantics, persistence, write authority, product claims, or external assurance.

## Global DoD
DECIDE-0 is satisfied only when all 13 REALITY inputs have one explicit decision; every decision traces to SCOPE and REALITY; fit is never converted into speculative rewrite; unverified capacity is routed to C3; delivery provenance to C2; AI eval/drift to C4; UIUX convergence is bound to C5 owner compression; E3/E4 remain external; runtime business behavior and write authority are unchanged; no release promotion occurs; and the next serial barrier is UIUX-CONVERGE-0.

## Intermediate DoD
1. **D0-I1 Input reconciliation** — 13 SCOPE dimensions, 13 REALITY verdicts, zero contradiction/hard contradiction, and all 14 UIUX work units are preserved losslessly.
2. **D0-I2 Decision closure** — each dimension has one bounded decision, evidence, rationale, forbidden claims, and a no-speculative-rewrite guard; all five decision classes are defined.
3. **D0-I3 Next-slice authority** — UIUX-CONVERGE-0 is authorized after merge; C1/C3/C4 become eligible; C2/C5 remain eligible; C5 is terminal before UIUX-CONVERGE-0 may be done.
4. **D0-I4 Epistemic boundary** — E3-HUMAN, E3-GOV and E4-DEPLOY remain external and no repository evidence silently closes them.
5. **D0-I5 Falsification** — baseline lattice passes; 197/197 independently materialized model mutants are rejected by the DECIDE validator; the same 197 semantic families are exercised across 1,000,000 deterministic multi-mutation trials, with zero survivor and zero harness error.

## Local DoD
- exact main base and model schema are explicit;
- the REALITY verdict vector is preserved exactly;
- every dimension carries rationale, evidence references, forbidden claims and `structuralRewriteAllowed=false`;
- UIUX keeps the 14 canonical work units and row/list registry default;
- conditional slices retain explicit eligibility and closure predicates;
- nine rejected architecture/pattern choices have evidence-based reopening conditions;
- claim boundaries forbid runtime/business-authority promotion;
- checker and saturation results are deterministic on the same candidate bytes.

## Decision classes
- `PRESERVE`: keep the current bounded architecture/contract.
- `CHANGE`: authorize a bounded implementation change already justified by evidence.
- `MEASURE_CONDITIONAL`: resolve an unverified envelope or conditional debt before stronger claims.
- `EXTERNAL_DEFER`: keep the claim outside repository authority.
- `REJECT`: explicitly reject a familiar but unjustified architecture/pattern, with evidence required to reopen it.

## Architectural decision
REALITY-0 found zero contradiction and zero hard structural contradiction. DECIDE-0 therefore preserves Node, vanilla UI, Store, SQLite and one composition root by default. It rejects a second business write store, mandatory PostgreSQL/object-storage core, event-sourcing rewrite, active-active HA/distributed queue, embedded identity authority, generic BPM, global final UI resolver, an eighth peer business process, and AI decision authority unless the recorded reopening condition is satisfied.

## UIUX decision
DECIDE-0 authorizes one `UIUX-CONVERGE-0` implementation barrier. The 14 work units remain implementation units, not governance barriers. The implementation must compress semantic/presentation ownership, preserve semantic order and epistemically material state, keep repeated registries row/list-first, and may not create a global late resolver. `C5-SEMANTIC-OWNER-COMPRESSION` must be terminal before UIUX-CONVERGE-0 can be declared done.

## Conditional work
After DECIDE-0, C1 compatibility contraction, C3 capacity contract and C4 AI eval/drift become eligible. C2 delivery provenance and C5 owner compression remain eligible from TRUTH-0. All C1-C5 must be `done` or evidence-backed `not-required` before S4-A6-CLOSE; C5 has the additional UIUX terminality requirement.

## Claim boundary
DECIDE-0 creates no enterprise-ready, legal/compliance, certification, market-fit, HA, RTO/RPO or production-effectiveness conclusion. Capacity remains C3; delivery provenance remains C2; AI eval/drift remains C4; owner compression remains C5; E3-HUMAN/E3-GOV/E4-DEPLOY stay external; GAP-020/GAP-021 remain open.
