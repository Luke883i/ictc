# ICTC 1.2 Market Candidate — Convergent Kernel DoD

## Goal
Preserve seven process-specific compliance procedures while making every layer of ICTC recognizably one product. Complexity belongs below the first screen. The visible grammar is shared; lifecycle, decision checkpoints, evidence and exit semantics remain process-specific.

## Global DoD
100% means **all** A01–A20 plus existing G1–G14 are green on the same exact PR head. No weighted score compensates a red gate.

## Local procedure DoD
Every process must answer with the same first-screen grammar: why am I here; current governed state; what needs attention; one primary action; remaining human decision; which evidence/version will remain. The answers are process-specific: RN source/change-centric; EC case/timeline-centric; AO registry-centric; MC standard/control-centric; AP work/verification-centric; RC scenario/rating-centric; AR question/response-centric.

## Sub-local record DoD
- L1 orientation: label, state, attention, primary action.
- L2 work: process-specific fields and next transition.
- L3 record: scope, authority, owner, relations, version/freshness.
- L4 decision/evidence: checkpoint, evidence bindings, limitations, AI provenance.
- L5 technical trace: receipts, hashes, audit events, raw epistemic payload/export.

## Evidence boundary
DoD proves bounded internal consistency and executable behavior. Model saturation and browser/runtime CI are E2 evidence. They do not substitute for E4 independent human comprehension, legal assessment, external security review, certification or a production deployment assessment.
