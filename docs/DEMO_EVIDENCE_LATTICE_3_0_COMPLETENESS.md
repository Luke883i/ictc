# DEMO Evidence Lattice 3.0 — completeness contract

## Scope of completeness

`Complete` is bounded, not universal. The DEMO is closed against the ontology and epistemic vocabulary ICTC can currently represent for a typical one-year SME operating scenario. It is not a claim that every possible SME, sector, legal fact or technology is represented.

The closed SME ontology requires explicit representation of: organization and roles; technology estate; information/privacy; organization and third parties; security/compliance objects; normative universe; monitoring sources; operational events; controls/tests; mappings; actions; risks; assurance; evidence; operating time; cross-procedure relations.

The epistemic completeness contract requires all canonical ICTC families visible in the current runtime projection: `observed`, `derived`, `proposed`, `decided`, `attested`. The evidence lattice additionally requires E0 source, E1 observation, E2 governed record, E3 human decision, E4 verified outcome and E5 annual portfolio view.

## Exclusive DEMO loading

Suite 2.2 remains the sole native write authority. Seeding is accepted only on empty business state; legacy DEMO markers and partial Suite 2.2 markers fail closed. On a clean isolated runtime, all seven business collections, catalog, control tests and principals must equal the Suite 2.2 build rather than append to previous DEMO data. Re-running after completion is idempotent.

## Real lattice criterion

The lattice is more than a list of records when all of the following hold simultaneously: every native subject is represented exactly once; all graph edges resolve; subject membership binds organization, native procedure and operating time; cross-procedure typed correlations exist; correlation never transfers authority or becomes causation; event time and knowledge time remain distinct; unknowns remain explicit; an E5 annual portfolio node derives from, rather than replaces, the lower-level graph.

## Falsification

The dedicated exact-head rail validates the real Suite 2.2 graph and then executes 1,000,000 deterministic mutations of a compact invariant projection derived from that graph across ontology, epistemic and semantic axes. The campaign is repository/model evidence. It is not evidence from one million companies, legal analyses, users or independent full-graph clones.
