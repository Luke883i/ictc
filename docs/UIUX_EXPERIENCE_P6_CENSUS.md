**Non-claim:** source contracts and browser automation do not prove that representative users find ICTC beautiful, understandable or efficient. Those remain E3-HUMAN.

## Convergence rule

P6 does not add another visual theme or global final resolver. It compresses the mounted experience through the current local owners and a small common grammar: Chrome, SurfaceIdentity, SurfacePurposeBoundary, AttentionStrip, ControlRail, RecordRow, PrimaryAction, StateChip, ProgressiveDetail and EvidenceConfigurationRow.

The global law is lossless: state, subject identity, authority/owner, next action, material boundary, basis/version/due/provenance may wrap or move into immediately adjacent progressive detail, but may not disappear when they change a decision.

## Home mount defect and solution

The pre-P6 bootstrap had three temporal Home titles: static HTML `Porta il lavoro di compliance alla prossima decisione.`, then `stable-shell.js` wrote `Cosa richiede attenzione?`, then A6-UX3 wrote `Integrated Compliance Tower Control`. This is a real temporal ownership defect, not a loading-performance illusion.

P6 chooses the product identity already expected by the A6 browser contract as the single canonical H1: `Integrated Compliance Tower Control`. The static DOM starts with that title; `stable-shell.js` composes data and priorities without rewriting it; the A6-UX3 compatibility layer keeps operational annotations but no longer rewrites Home title copy. Expected visible title transitions after first paint: zero.

## Global design decisions

- Header gradient becomes lighter while retaining white-text contrast; footer remains slightly darker than header but no longer near-black.
- Landing surfaces use a light blue-grey tint that is darker than white cards and far lighter than chrome.
- Repeated controls preserve 44 px minimum target height; compact record rows remain 52–64 px when content permits.
- Process-directory CTA geometry remains uniform and is formalized at 8.75 rem minimum width.
- `Vai a…` becomes `Vai a` with a search icon. Search-result metadata expresses kind/code/state once: no `Procedura · RN-01 · Procedura` or `Azione · Azioni · stato`.
- AI readiness uses an immediate icon plus short visible labels (`AI pronta`, `AI non attiva`); precise configuration detail remains in `title` and `aria-label`.
- Color is never a compliance verdict. AI-ready/attention color communicates only local feature readiness.
