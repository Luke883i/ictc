# ICTC Control Tower V2 — GRC convergence

V2 turns the V1 semantic kernel into a usable GRC operating layer without adding parallel authorities.

## Clarified product outcome
The product adds five planned processes from the canonical bundle: AO-01 Object Inventory, MC-01 Declared Mapping & Coverage, AP-01 Action Plan, RC-01 Compliance Risk, and AR-01 Assurance. FI-01 remains a governed subprocess of monitoring. New GRC vocabulary is modeled as objects and relations before it is modeled as new processes.

## Global DoD
- one ontology authority, one relation grammar, one process registry, one decision projection, one epistemic projection, one evidence graph, one active UI composition;
- AO/MC/AP/RC/AR available end-to-end;
- every write returns a receipt and survives readback;
- AI only proposes; human checkpoints bind accepted mapping, priority, risk rating and assurance answers;
- heatmap and consolidated risk metrics use human-reviewed ratings by default;
- coverage is bounded to a declared universe and keeps gap / not-applicable / unresolved distinct;
- no business process becomes a permanent top-level tab;
- role-scoped search/download and evidence authorization remain same-as-read;
- each process has one primary CTA, progressive disclosure, persistent todo state, receipt/evidence feedback and a next action;
- 10k+ semantic/UX saturation produces no unexpected primitive or dead-end journey;
- V2 ready-to-use is experimental product readiness, not enterprise production readiness or certification.

## Slice sequence
1. V2-S1 GRC foundation: registry-extension, object taxonomy, shared relation grammar and generic GRC surface contract.
2. V2-S2 AO-01: governed object registry, owner/source authority, criticality and relations.
3. V2-S3 MC-01: declared universe, AI mapping proposals, human decision, bounded coverage.
4. V2-S4 AP-01: persistent remediation work, owner/due/status, AI priority proposal and human adoption.
5. V2-S5 RC-01: risk scenario, human-reviewed dimensions, heatmap, control/action links.
6. V2-S6 AR-01: questionnaire intake, AI decomposition/draft, evidence links, human approval.
7. V2-S7 UX/dashboard weld: professional minimal entrypoint, consolidated values, terminology/focus audit and all-service saturation.

## Weld rule
Each slice reuses the same store mutation/receipt mechanism, process registry, generic GRC workspace and evidence graph. A later slice may extend data and projections but must not introduce a second router, local relation vocabulary, decision table, epistemic enum or evidence store.
